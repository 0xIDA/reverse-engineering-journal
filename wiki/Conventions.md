# Conventions

- **Entries:** `entries/<target-name>/` — kebab-case, one folder per target, updated in place; dates live in the header table.
- **Committed:** writeups, notes, scripts, and explicitly-shared IDA databases.
- **Local-only:** `artifacts/` (dumps, samples) and `ida/` contents unless a database is explicitly shared.
- **Dead ends get written down** — the Process section records hypotheses and failures, not just wins.
- **Notes link back** to their source entry; entries link forward to their notes.
- **Wiki:** markdown source lives in `wiki/` in the main repo; publishing = copying these files into the [wiki repo](https://github.com/0xIDA/reverse-engineering-journal.wiki) (Home, `_Sidebar`, Entries, Notes, Conventions).
